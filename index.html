Mathematical choices are also political. Representing a spherical object on a two-dimensional surface is not an easy task. So much so that there are hundreds of map projections, each choosing to prioritize different properties, such as shape, distance, or area.

Drag and move any shape to observe its distortions across the projections. Start with Greenland and Africa, add more with the controls above: a specific country, a whole continent, or a plain geometric shape to see how the globe itself warps them....
