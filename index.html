Representing a spherical object on a two-dimensional surface is not an easy task. So much so that there are hundreds of map projections, each choosing to prioritize different properties, as shape, distance, or area.

Drag and move any shape to observe its distortions across the projections. Starts with Greenland and Africa — add more with the controls above: a specific country, a whole continent, or a plain geometric shape (square, triangle, circle) to see how the globe itself warps them.
